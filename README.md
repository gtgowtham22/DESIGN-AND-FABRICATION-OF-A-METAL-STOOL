# DESIGN AND FABRICATION OF A METAL STOOL

### Workshop Fabrication, Welding and Structural Assembly

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on the design and fabrication of a simple metal stool frame using metal sections and welded joints.

The fabricated prototype consists of a rectangular upper frame supported by four legs, with additional cross and diagonal/triangular members provided to improve rigidity and structural support.

The project was carried out as a practical workshop activity demonstrating the complete fabrication workflow from measurement and cutting to assembly, tack welding, final welding and inspection.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Design a compact four-legged stool frame.
* Prepare and position the metal members accurately.
* Create rigid welded joints at the required intersections.
* Provide sufficient structural support for the seat area.
* Inspect the completed frame for alignment and stability.

---

## 🏗️ Main Structural Components

| Component                  | Function                                   |
| -------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| Upper rectangular frame    | Supports the seat and connects the legs    |
| Four legs                  | Transfer the seating load to the floor     |
| Cross members              | Improve rigidity and maintain spacing      |
| Diagonal/triangular braces | Reduce side movement and improve stiffness |
| Welded joints              | Permanently connect the members            |

## ⚙️ Fabrication Procedure

### Step 1 — Measurement and Marking

The required metal members were measured and marked according to the planned stool dimensions.

### Step 2 — Cutting and Preparation

The metal sections were cut/prepared to the required lengths, and contact surfaces were cleaned.

### Step 3 — Upper Frame Assembly

The perimeter members were placed together to form the rectangular seat-supporting frame.

### Step 4 — Tack Welding

Small tack welds were applied at the corners and intersections to hold the frame temporarily.

### Step 5 — Leg Positioning

Four vertical/inclined legs were positioned beneath the upper frame and checked for symmetry.

### Step 6 — Reinforcement Installation

Additional cross and diagonal/triangular members were positioned at selected locations to improve stiffness.

### Step 7 — Final Welding

After alignment was checked, the structural joints were welded more completely.

### Step 8 — Cleaning and Inspection

Weld areas were cleaned as necessary and the completed frame was visually inspected.

---

## 🔥 Welding Process

### Tack Welding

Tack welding was used to hold the metal members in their intended locations before applying the final welds. This helped maintain the rectangular shape of the upper frame and the correct position of the four legs.

### Final Welding

After confirming the geometry, the joints were welded to create permanent connections at:

* Corners of the upper frame
* Leg-to-frame connections
* Selected reinforcement points

### Reinforcement Welding

Additional diagonal/triangular support members near the corners provide an additional load path and help reduce deformation or side movement of the stool during use.

---

## 🔍 Welding Quality Requirements

* Ensure clean contact between the members before welding.
* Maintain adequate fusion along the intended joint.
* Avoid excessive gaps between members.
* Remove loose slag and excessive spatter after welding.
* Inspect joints for visible cracks, undercut, porosity or incomplete weld areas.
* Repair any visibly inadequate joint before the stool is put into service.

---

## 📐 Dimensional Checks

The fabricated frame was checked for:

* Length and width of the upper seat frame
* Distance between the legs
* Symmetry of the footprint
* Level and squareness of the top frame
* Corresponding diagonal dimensions
* Proper floor contact of all four legs

---

## 🧪 Stability Inspection

The stool should be placed on a level floor and checked for rocking. Light manual force can be applied at the upper frame to observe unwanted movement.

Any unstable condition should be corrected before a person sits on the stool.

## 🔮 Future Improvements

* Add a properly sized and securely fixed seat surface.
* Provide rubber/plastic foot caps to improve floor contact and reduce slipping or floor damage.
* Grind sharp edges and weld spatter for safer handling.
* Apply anti-rust primer and a durable protective paint coating.
* Verify the final dimensions and material thickness in the project drawing.
* Carry out a controlled stability and load test appropriate to the intended use.
* Improve the seat with a cushion or ergonomic top if required.
